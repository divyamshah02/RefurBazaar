COOKIE POLICY

Effective Date: 20th April, 2026

Recarvit uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and to understand how users interact with the Platform.

7.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve functionality.

7.2 Types of Cookies We Use
  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the Platform to function. These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences (e.g., language, location).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users navigate the Platform (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track campaign performance.
7.3 Managing Cookies

You may control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may impair the functionality of the Platform. By continuing to use the Platform without changing your cookie settings, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this Policy.
