WARRANTY POLICY

Effective Date: 20th April, 2026

All products sold through Recarvit are covered by a 6-month warranty provided by GoWarranty, an independent third-party warranty service provider. This warranty is offered as a value-added service to Buyers and is subject to GoWarranty's own terms, conditions, and claims process.

5.1 Warranty Provider

Warranty services are administered exclusively by GoWarranty. Recarvit is not the warranty provider and does not process warranty claims directly.

  • Toll Free: 1800 123 868 686
  • Support Email: help@gowarranty.in
5.2 Warranty Duration

The warranty period is 6 (six) months from the date of delivery of the product to the Buyer, as confirmed by the logistics partner's delivery record.

5.3 What the Warranty Covers

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal usage conditions, including but not limited to:

  • Internal hardware component failures (e.g., display circuitry, motherboard components, charging modules).
  • Battery failure beyond normal degradation patterns as defined by GoWarranty.
  • Connectivity module failures (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular) not caused by physical damage.
  • Speaker, microphone, or camera malfunction arising from internal defects.
  • Button and port failures not caused by physical abuse or liquid ingress.
5.4 What the Warranty Does Not Cover

The following are explicitly excluded from warranty coverage (as per industry standards):

  • Physical damage including cracked screens, bent chassis, broken ports, or any impact damage.
  • Liquid or moisture damage.
  • Damage caused by unauthorised repair or modification.
  • Rooted or jailbroken devices.
  • Cosmetic defects such as scratches, dents, or discolouration consistent with or introduced after delivery.
  • Loss of data or software issues.
  • Accessories (chargers, cables, earphones) unless explicitly covered.
  • Damage caused by use outside the manufacturer's recommended operating conditions.
  • Consequential or incidental damages arising from product failure.
  • Products with tampered, removed, or unreadable IMEI/serial numbers.
5.5 How to Raise a Warranty Claim
  1. Contact GoWarranty directly via Toll Free 1800 123 868 686 or email help@gowarranty.in.
  2. Provide your Order ID, product IMEI/serial number, purchase date, and a detailed description of the issue.
  3. GoWarranty will guide you through their verification and claim process.
  4. Recarvit is not responsible for the outcome of warranty claims or the timeline of warranty servicing. All warranty decisions are final and at the discretion of GoWarranty.
5.6 Warranty Disclaimer

The warranty provided through GoWarranty is the sole and exclusive warranty applicable to products purchased on Recarvit. Refurbazaar Private Limited makes no additional warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose, beyond what is offered through GoWarranty's service agreement.
