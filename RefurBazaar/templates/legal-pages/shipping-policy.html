SHIPPING POLICY

Effective Date: 20th April, 2026

6.1 Shipping Coverage

Recarvit currently ships to serviceable pin codes across India. Serviceability is subject to change based on logistics partner availability. During checkout, the Platform will confirm whether delivery is available at your pin code.

6.2 Dispatch & Delivery Timeline
  • Sellers are required to dispatch orders within 24-48 hours of order confirmation.
  • Standard delivery timelines are 4-7 business days from the date of dispatch, depending on the delivery location.
  • Delivery to remote or non-metro areas may take up to 10 business days.

Recarvit does not guarantee delivery by a specific date and is not liable for delays caused by logistics partners, weather, or circumstances beyond our control.

6.3 Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if any, are displayed at checkout before Order confirmation. Recarvit may offer free shipping on select products or during promotional periods. Shipping charges are non-refundable except in cases where the delivery was not attempted.

6.4 Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, tracking details will be sent to your registered email and/or mobile number.

6.5 Failed Deliveries

If a delivery attempt fails due to incorrect address provided by the Buyer, Buyer unavailability after three delivery attempts, or refusal to accept the shipment, the order will be returned to the Seller. Re-delivery may be attempted at an additional charge. No refund will be issued for failed deliveries attributable to the Buyer.

6.6 Damaged in Transit

If a product is visibly damaged upon delivery, the Buyer must report the matter to Recarvit within 24 hours via info@recarvit.com with photographic evidence. Acceptance of a visibly damaged package without notation constitutes acknowledgment of receipt in satisfactory condition.

6.7 Cash on Delivery (COD)

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available on select products and pin codes. A partial advance payment may be required at the time of order placement to confirm a COD order. Refusal to accept a COD delivery without a valid reason will result in the Buyer bearing all associated logistics charges and may lead to restriction of COD privileges on their account. COD orders refused at delivery will not be eligible for a refund of the advance amount paid.
