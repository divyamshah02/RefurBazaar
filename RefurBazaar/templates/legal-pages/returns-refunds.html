RETURN & REFUND POLICY

Effective Date: 20th April, 2026

Recarvit strives to ensure Buyer satisfaction. However, given the nature of refurbished electronics, returns are accepted under specific, verifiable circumstances as defined in this Policy. All return requests are carefully reviewed and are subject to fulfilment at Recarvit's sole discretion.

4.1 Return Window

Return requests must be raised within 7 (seven) calendar days from the date of delivery. Requests raised after this window will not be accepted under any circumstances, and no exceptions will be made regardless of the reason cited.

4.2 Conditions for a Valid Return

A return request will only be considered valid if ALL of the following conditions are satisfied simultaneously:

  1. The return request is raised within 7 calendar days of delivery as confirmed by the logistics partner's delivery timestamp.
  2. The product has not been used beyond initial functional verification (i.e., not used as a daily device).
  3. The product is in the exact condition in which it was received — no additional scratches, dents, missing accessories, or physical alterations of any kind.
  4. The original packaging (if provided), all accessories (charger, cable, adapters), and documentation shipped with the product are returned intact and undamaged.
  5. The IMEI or serial number of the returned device exactly matches the IMEI or serial number listed in the original Order.
  6. The product has not been rooted, jailbroken, flashed with custom firmware, or subjected to any unauthorised software modifications.
  7. The product shows no evidence of liquid damage, physical impact, or deliberate tampering beyond what was documented at the time of delivery.
  8. The Buyer provides clear unboxing video evidence (recorded at the time of opening the package) demonstrating the issue. Returns without unboxing video proof will not be processed.
  9. The issue reported is a functional defect that was pre-existing at the time of delivery and is verifiably not caused by post-delivery user handling or environmental factors.
  10. A formal return request has been submitted via the Platform's return portal or by emailing info@recarvit.com with full Order details, photographic evidence, and a detailed description of the issue.
  11. The product is not listed as a non-returnable category (see Section 4.3).
  12. The Buyer's account is in good standing with no prior return abuse flagged by Recarvit's quality team.
4.3 Non-Returnable Categories

The following are explicitly excluded from returns:

  • Products where the stated issue is purely cosmetic and consistent with the listed grading tier.
  • Products with IMEI/serial number mismatches on return.
  • Products where physical damage has occurred post-delivery.
  • Products that have been used for more than 72 hours post-delivery (verified via system telemetry or usage logs where available).
  • Products where the reported issue is software-related and can be resolved through a factory reset.
  • Products with broken seals, missing accessories, or repackaged in non-original packaging.
  • Accessories and peripherals sold separately (e.g., chargers, cables, headphones).
  • Products purchased during special sale events marked as "Final Sale" or "No Return".
  • Products where the defect was clearly disclosed in the product listing (e.g., listed as non-functional speaker).
  • Any return request where the Buyer has previously initiated a chargeback for the same Order.
  • Change of mind, personal preference, or the Buyer no longer requiring the product.
  • Cosmetic wear, scratches, or marks consistent with the product's listed grade (Fair / Good / Superb) - what you see in the grade description is what you get.
  • Minor variations in colour, finish, or appearance from product images, as images are representational.
  • Delayed delivery due to logistics or circumstances beyond Recarvit's control.
4.4 Grounds for Return Rejection

Recarvit reserves the right to reject a return request, even within the return window, if:

  • The returned product does not match what was originally dispatched (Variant/IMEI/serial number verification will be conducted).
  • The condition of the returned product is worse than its listed grade, suggesting post-delivery damage.
  • The Buyer fails to cooperate with the verification process, including not providing an unboxing video.
  • The return is deemed to be an attempt to swap a damaged or different device.
  • The stated reason for return is not substantiated upon inspection.
  • There is evidence of software manipulation, rooting, or unlocking.
  • Multiple return requests have been raised by the same Buyer account in a short period (indicative of return abuse).
4.5 Return Process
  1. Submit your return request via the Platform portal or by emailing info@recarvit.com with your Order ID, reason for return, and supporting evidence (photos/video).
  2. Recarvit's quality team will review your request within 5-7 business days and communicate approval or rejection.
  3. If approved, a reverse pickup will be scheduled. The product must be securely packed and handed to the logistics partner within 48 hours of pickup scheduling.
  4. Upon receipt, the returned product will undergo a mandatory quality inspection (typically 5-7 business days).
  5. If the inspection confirms the valid defect, Recarvit will proceed with a replacement or refund as per Section 4.6.
  6. If the inspection reveals that the product does not meet return conditions, the product will be shipped back to the Buyer at their cost and no refund or replacement will be issued.
4.6 Replacement vs. Refund

Recarvit's default resolution for an approved return is a replacement. The following order of resolution applies:

  • Replacement First: If an equivalent or better product (same model, same or superior grade) is available in inventory, Recarvit will dispatch a replacement. The Buyer shall not have the right to insist on a refund solely because they prefer one over a replacement.
  • Refund as Last Resort: A monetary refund will only be issued if (a) no replacement stock exists for the same model (b) Recarvit determines in its sole discretion that a refund is the appropriate resolution. Refunds are not available on demand.
4.7 Refund Deductions

Refunds are subject to applicable deductions including but not limited to logistics, payment processing, and handling charges, except in cases where the return is solely attributable to Recarvit's or the Seller's error.

4.8 Refund Timeline

Approved refunds will be credited back to the original payment method within 7-10 business days after successful quality inspection of the returned product.

4.9 Order Cancellation

Buyers may cancel an order without penalty within 2 hours of placement, provided the order has not yet been dispatched. Post-dispatch cancellations will be treated as a return request under Section 4. If a Seller fails to fulfil an order within the stipulated dispatch window, the order will be automatically cancelled and a full refund issued to the Buyer with no deductions.
