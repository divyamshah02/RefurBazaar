PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 20th April, 2026

Refurbazaar Private Limited ("Company") is committed to protecting the privacy of its users in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDPA"). The Data Fiduciary for the purposes of this Policy is Refurbazaar Private Limited, operating under the brand name Recarvit.

2.1 Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Full name, email address, phone number, and shipping/billing address.
  • Government-issued identification (for Sellers completing KYC).
  • Bank account or payment details for transaction processing.

Automatically Collected Data

  • IP address, device type, browser type, and operating system.
  • Pages visited, time spent on the Platform, and clickstream data.
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Cookie Policy).

Transaction Data

  • Order history, payment records, product listings, and communication logs.
2.2 How We Use Your Information
  • To process orders, payments, and facilitate deliveries.
  • To verify Seller identities and manage marketplace compliance.
  • To communicate order updates, customer support responses, and platform notifications.
  • To improve platform performance, conduct analytics, and personalise user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our Terms.
  • To send promotional communications where you have provided consent (opt-out available).
2.3 Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with:

  • Logistics partners for order fulfilment and delivery.
  • Payment gateways and financial institutions for transaction processing.
  • GoWarranty (our third-party warranty service provider) for warranty registration.
  • Government authorities or law enforcement when legally required.
  • Analytics and technology service providers under confidentiality obligations.

Ecoreco is not a data recipient and has no access to any personal data collected by Recarvit. The brand association does not constitute a data sharing arrangement of any kind.

2.4 Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as required to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy. Transaction records are retained for a minimum of 7 years in compliance with Indian tax and accounting laws. You may request deletion of your data subject to legal and contractual obligations.

2.5 Your Rights Under DPDPA
  • Right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure of your personal data, subject to applicable exceptions.
  • Right to grievance redressal through our Grievance Officer.
  • Right to withdraw consent for processing based on consent at any time.
2.6 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL encryption, access controls, and regular security assessments to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

2.7 Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed to children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.
