Our Process

One Asset. Multiple Possibilities.

Every retired device is carefully evaluated to maximise its value while supporting a sustainable circular economy.

1

Collection & Evaluation

Your devices are collected and assessed to determine suitability for refurbishment or responsible recycling.

2

Secure Data Erasure

Professional data sanitisation permanently removes all confidential business information before further processing.

3

Refurbishment

Eligible devices are cleaned, repaired and upgraded where needed, restoring them to dependable working condition.

4

Quality Certification

Every refurbished device undergoes rigorous functional testing and quality inspection before resale approval.

5

Redeployment or Recycling

Certified devices get a second life through Recarvit; non-repairable assets are responsibly recycled to compliance standards.
