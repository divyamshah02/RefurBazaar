The 45-Point Quality Check

Power

Battery health (85%+), Charging port, Cable functionality.

Display

Touch response, No dead pixels, True Tone verification.

Camera

Focus, Zoom, Flash, Front/Back lens clarity.

Connectivity

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GPS, FaceID/TouchID sensors.

Audio & Acoustics

Ensuring speakers, microphones, and headphone jacks are crystal clear.

Sensors & Buttons

Checking that biometric sensors (fingerprint/Face ID), haptics & physical volume/power buttons are fully responsive.
