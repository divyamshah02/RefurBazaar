The Recarvit Mission

India has millions of people who want an iPhone. A MacBook. A Samsung flagship. But new prices put these devices out of reach for most. At the same time, millions of perfectly functional devices are traded in, discarded, and wasted every year — creating an e-waste crisis and a massive opportunity.

Recarvit exists at that intersection.

The name says it: Recarve + IT. We take existing devices and recarve them — restoring them to their finest form so they find a second life with someone who values them. Every device on Recarvit is certified, graded honestly, and backed by a warranty. No guesswork. No compromise.

Our Grading, Explained.

We don't hide imperfections — we grade them. Every grade is fully tested, certified, and warranted.

Appearance matters

Superb

"Like it just left the box."

  • Screen: None visible to the naked eye.
  • Body: Near-perfect condition.
  • Battery: 90%+ Minimum Capacity guaranteed.
  • Warranty: 6 Months Hardware included.
  • Savings: Up to 35% off new price.
Best value

Good

"Well-loved, perfectly functional."

  • Screen: Light or faint micro-scratches.
  • Body: Minor wear, scuffs or tiny nicks.
  • Battery: 85%+ Minimum Capacity guaranteed.
  • Warranty: 6 Months Hardware included.
  • Savings: Up to 50% off new price.
Maximum savings

Fair

"Built for performance, not display."

  • Screen: Clearly visible scratches, but no cracks.
  • Body: Visible wear, noticeable dents or scratches.
  • Battery: 80%+ Minimum Capacity guaranteed.
  • Warranty: 6 Months Hardware included.
  • Savings: Up to 65% off new price.
